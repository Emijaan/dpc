METHOD A • RECOMMENDED FOR ANDROID 12 / 13 / 14 / 15+ (OPPO, VIVO, SAMSUNG, XIAOMI)

Step 1: Scan Google Test DPC Setup QR (Welcome Screen)

  1. Factory-reset the customer phone. On the "Hello / Welcome" screen, tap the same spot 6–7 times.
  2. Scan this Step 1 QR Code and connect to Wi-Fi.
  3. When asked "How will you use this phone?", select USE FOR WORK ONLY (never select Work & Personal).
  4. Once on the home screen, open Google Play Store → Play Protect → Settings and turn OFF Scan apps with Play Protect.

METHOD A • STEP 2 & 3

Step 2: Install AK DeviceLock & Transfer Ownership

  1. Open Chrome / QR Scanner on the customer phone and scan this Step 2 QR Code to download & install AK DeviceLock (akdevicelock.apk).
  2. Important: After installing, tap OPEN once (so Android activates the package), then switch to Test DPC.
  3. In Test DPC → search transfer → tap "Transfer ownership to component".
  4. Select:
    {{ admin_component }}
    and tap TRANSFER.
  5. AK DeviceLock opens automatically as Device Owner, blocks Factory Reset, and registers with the server automatically!
